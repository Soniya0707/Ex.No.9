## Ex.No.9 Exploration of Prompting Techniques for Video Generation

## Date: 03/06/2026

## Reg. No: 212223060268

## Aim:

To explore and understand the various prompting techniques used for generating videos using AI models.

## Algorithm

1.Familiarize yourself with video generation models.

2.Create simple prompts for video generation. 

3.Experiment with detailed prompts. 

4.Add time and motion elements.

5.Test different video styles.

6.Iterate and adjust prompts.

7.Generate multiple versions.

8.Save and compare outputs.

## Procedure

## 1. Familiarize Yourself with Video Generation Models

## Explore AI tools capable of generating videos from text prompts:

1.Runway Gen-2 

2.Synthesia

3.Pictory 

4.DeepBrain AI Understand their capabilities, limitations, output duration, style options, and supported features before starting.

## 2. Create Simple Prompts for Video Generation

Begin with basic prompts to observe how AI interprets simple actions.

## Example prompt:

A cinematic anime-style scene of a young Japanese girl in a vibrant red floral kimono playing badminton in a serene traditional garden, surrounded by bamboo trees, stone lanterns, and wooden architecture.

Soft golden sunlight, shallow depth of field, smooth slow-motion movement, realistic fabric physics, and dreamy atmospheric lighting inspired by Makoto Shinkai aesthetics.

## 3. Experiment with More Detailed Prompts

Gradually increase the complexity by adding environmental details and actions.

## Example Prompt:

A cinematic slow-motion video of a young athletic woman playing tennis on an outdoor court during golden hour, wearing a neon green and white sports outfit, with dynamic racket swings and smooth body movement.

Warm sunset lighting filtering through trees, realistic motion blur, shallow depth of field, soft lens flare, and professional sports-commercial aesthetics with ultra-natural colors.

## Output video:

 https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LvOgK_8FI9peIwS0yKwhvTl8hBXKLmls/view?usp=drivesdk

## 4. Add Time and Motion Elements

Include elements such as transitions, time-lapse, or camera movement.

Dramatic volumetric lighting, warm orange and purple sky tones, realistic shadows, soft atmospheric haze, and ultra-smooth camera motion creating an epic cinematic landscape feel.
